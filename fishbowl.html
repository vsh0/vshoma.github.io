Fishbowl Animation

This is a second-year project for my 3D modelling module. I used Blender to create a fishbowl scene. I modelled the fishbowl and the fish in it and added a shell and plant from the free asset store due to time constraints. I learned to animate camera movements, make water and glass textures, and UV unwrapping.
