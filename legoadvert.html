Animal crossing Lego Advert

This was a first year project for my graphic design module. For this project, we were tasked with creating a video advertisement and editing it in Photoshop. Using photoshop for video editing rather than image editing was a unique challenge. I made my advert for the Lego playset "Bunnie's Outdoor Adventure". I created a cardboard backdrop to film the set and edited a splash screen together in Photoshop, following Animal Crossing's branding.
