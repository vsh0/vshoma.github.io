Mossbudd Branding

This was a first-year project for my graphic design module. We were tasked with creating a branding scheme for a business of our choice. I decided to create branding my art business. I made a logo, poster, business card, brochure, and tri-fold leaflet. I used Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, to create the designs and used Medibang paint pro for the art! I wanted to solidify a cohesive look for my branding to keep my online presence consistent. I also got to use my design work for my convention stalls.
