Music Visualisation

This is a second-year project for my 3D modelling module. I used Blender to create a music visualiser for the song "Onward to our own Futures" from Pokemon Black and White. I used a music visualiser extension for Blender to make the elements in the scene move to the music. I created the 3D logo from an image of the Pokemon logo.
